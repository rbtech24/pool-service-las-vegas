Quick answer for Las Vegas homeowners

Run long enough to turn the water over and support the filter—especially after wind or parties. That is the practical core of pool pump runtime in a desert climate—not a random pile of store chemicals.

What to do this week about pool pump runtime

  1. Step 1. Aim for a full turnover plus filter time—not the shortest timer setting.
  2. Step 2. Increase runtime after dust or heavy swimming.
  3. Step 3. On variable-speed pumps, use a longer low-speed window instead of one short blast.
  4. Step 4. Listen for new noises when the pump starts.
  5. Step 5. If water stays dull, check filter pressure before only adding more chemicals.

FAQs about pool pump runtime in Las Vegas

Is 4 hours of pump time enough in summer?
Often no for a typical Las Vegas pool. Many homes need more circulation in peak heat.
Will longer runtime raise my bill a lot?
Variable-speed pumps keep costs down when programmed well. Cloudiness from short runs costs more in cleanups.
Should the pump run at night?
Night runtime helps in heat and can be quieter for neighbors—ask what fits your HOA and equipment.
