Saltwater vs. Chlorine Pools in the Valley

Salt isn’t maintenance-free — especially with hard desert water.

Saltwater pools dominate many newer Las Vegas builds, but salt is not “chlorine-free,” and it is not maintenance-free in hard desert water. This comparison helps homeowners choose and maintain either system with eyes open — including when saltwater pool service, weekly pool cleaning, and pool equipment repair enter the picture.

What salt systems actually do

A salt chlorinator electrolyzes dissolved salt into chlorine on site. Guests often prefer the feel. You still need balanced pH, alkalinity, calcium, stabilizer in range, and a working cell. When the cell scales or the board faults, chlorine production drops and algae gets a vote. That is saltwater pool service territory, not a reason to abandon weekly care.
