Quick answer for Las Vegas homeowners

What to do this week about monsoon pool cleaning

  1. Step 1. Empty baskets after storms even if service is tomorrow.
  2. Step 2. Retest chlorine—rain dilutes and debris consumes it.
  3. Step 3. Watch filter pressure after muddy runoff.
  4. Step 4. Do not assume a covered pool is fine without a check.
  5. Step 5. Call if the water turns overnight after a big storm.

How professional Las Vegas pool service helps

A solid weekly pool cleaning visit is more than a quick skim. Your tech should brush, empty baskets, test the water properly, balance chemicals for the week ahead, and glance at the equipment pad.
