What to do this week about pool chemical service

  1. Step 1. If you DIY, test before you pour—never dose by smell or color alone.
  2. Step 2. Avoid stacking clarifier, phosphate remover, and shock on the same day without a plan.
  3. Step 3. Store chemicals cool and dry; heat wrecks product quality.
  4. Step 4. Know when to stop DIY: green water, dead equipment, or recurring cloudiness.
  5. Step 5. Consider weekly pro service if you miss weeks or hate testing.

How professional Las Vegas pool service helps

A solid weekly pool cleaning visit is more than a quick skim. Your tech should brush, empty baskets, test the water properly, balance chemicals for the week ahead, and glance at the equipment pad.

If the water has already turned, you may need green pool cleanup. If the pump, filter, or salt system is the weak link, pool equipment repair comes first—chemicals cannot fix a system that will not circulate.
