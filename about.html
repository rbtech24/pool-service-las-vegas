About Pool Service Las Vegas

Strip-side standards. Backyard-clear water.

A Las Vegas-first pool brand

Pool Service Las Vegas started with a simple annoyance: too many route companies treat the valley like a mild coastal market. It isn’t. Heat, dust, hard water, and weekend guest spikes need a different rhythm.

We’re a local crew built for residential routes — clear communication, honest recovery timelines, and water that stays swimmable when the forecast says 112°.

You’ll deal with people who understand gate codes, HOA quiet hours, salt cells in hard water, and the difference between a quick skim and a real weekly program.

  • Licensed & insured Nevada operations
  • Residential-first routes (select small commercial)
  • Honest scopes — weekly vs. repair vs. recovery
  • Photo notes available for traveling owners
